> Google requires the page to name the app and the developer as they appear on Play.

**Jeepney Rush** by **[developer name as shown on Google Play]**

## Delete it in the game (fastest)
1. Open Jeepney Rush.
2. Tap **Account**.
3. Scroll to **Privacy** and tap **DELETE ACCOUNT**, then confirm twice.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Can't open the game?
E-mail **[your e-mail]** from the address linked to your account (or include your friend
code, shown on the Account screen) with the subject **"Delete my Jeepney Rush account"**.
We delete it within **30 days** and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted
Your sign-in (e-mail), display name, friend code, coins, gems, jeepneys, upgrades, scores,
missions, achievements, friends and purchase history.

## What is kept
Google Play purchase tokens, with no link to you, so the same purchase can't be claimed
twice. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases.
