Jeepney Rush ("the game") is made by [your name or studio] ("we"). This policy explains
what information the game collects and how it is used.

## What we collect
* **Account information.** When you play, the game creates an anonymous guest account. If
  you choose to save your progress with an e-mail address, we store that e-mail address.
* **Game data.** Your display name, friend code, coins, gems, jeepneys, upgrades, scores,
  missions, achievements and friends list, so your progress is saved and leaderboards work.
* **Purchases.** For in-app purchases we store the Google Play order id, product and
  purchase token so we can confirm the purchase and give you the items. We never see your
  payment details.
* **Advertising.** The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's
  advertising id, IP address and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads.
  See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
* **Diagnostics.** Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance reports.

## How we use it
To run the game, save your progress, show leaderboards and friends, deliver purchases,
show ads, prevent cheating and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information.

## Where it is stored
Game data is stored with our backend provider Supabase ([region, e.g. Singapore]). Data is
sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## How long we keep it
As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted
immediately, except purchase tokens kept without any link to you to prevent fraud.

## Your choices
* **Delete your account** in the game: Account ▸ Privacy ▸ DELETE ACCOUNT, or follow
  [link to your account-deletion page].
* **Ads personalisation:** Android Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads ▸ Delete advertising ID.
* You can ask for a copy or correction of your data by e-mailing us.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we
will delete it.
