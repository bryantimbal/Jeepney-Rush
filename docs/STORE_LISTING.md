## Full description (max 4000)

Pasada na, boss! Take the wheel of a real Filipino jeepney and drive the busiest roads of the Philippines. Dodge traffic, stop for passengers, collect fares and keep your riders happy in this colourful driving game.

🚍 BE THE DRIVER
• Stop at the right sakayan, let passengers board and hear them pass the fare forward: "Bayad po!"
• Drop riders off before they miss their stop, or they'll start shouting "PARA! PARA!"
• Keep your driver rating high – skip too many stops and your passengers will panic
• Watch out for MMDA and police checkpoints, red lights, potholes, speed humps and floods
• Refuel at the gas station (and hear your driver complain about the diesel price!)

🗺️ 7 ROUTES ACROSS THE PHILIPPINES
• Manila Streets – sari-sari stores, tangled wires and heavy traffic
• EDSA – the MRT overhead, buses, malls and the worst rush hour in the country
• Cebu City – heritage downtown and the seaside
• Baguio – pine trees, Session Road and mountain roads
• Provincial, Beach and Mountain Province routes – rice fields, coconut trees and winding roads
Every route has its own stops, signs and traffic enforcers.

🌇 A LIVING ROAD
• Day turns to sunset and night, with street lights and glowing jeepney lights
• Rain and typhoons with working windshield wipers
• Barkers calling "Cubao! Cubao! Sakay na!", street vendors, tricycles at the corners, people, dogs and birds
• Switch between the chase camera and a real driver's-seat view with an old-style speedometer

🎨 13 JEEPNEYS TO COLLECT
From the Pilipinas Classic to the Modern Jeepney, Fiesta Edition, Neon Street Racer and the loud Patok jeepneys of Cogeo and Antipolo – complete with sound-trip music and the "HILA KUNG BABABA" pull cord.

🔧 UPGRADE AND COMPETE
• Upgrade the engine, brakes, armour, passenger capacity and more
• Power-ups: magnet, shield, turbo, double coins and repair kits
• Daily and weekly missions, achievements and a daily challenge
• Global, weekly and friends leaderboards – add friends with your friend code
• Play offline any time; your progress syncs when you're back online

Kapit lang, mga pasahero – it's time to rush!

Jeepney Rush is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases.
