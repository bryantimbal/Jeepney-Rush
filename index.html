PAANO MAGLARO

  • Swipe ◀ ▶ (or ← → / A D) to change lanes.
  • Swipe ▲ (↑ / W / Space) to jump over cones, barriers and potholes.
  • Swipe ▼ (↓ / S) to brake hard — or slam down while jumping.

  • You have 3 hearts. Buses, trucks and cars can't be jumped.
